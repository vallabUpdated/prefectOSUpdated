ONE SPINE, FOUR JOBS

Not another dashboard. An operating system.

Everything an agent does passes through one spine. The spine has four jobs, and none of them involves trusting the agent's good intentions.

📖

The rulebook

Deny-by-default allowlists per agent — actions and resources, written by you. Unknown agent, unknown action, unknown target: refused before execution.

✋

The human gate

Money movement, customer contact, master-data edits — always held for a named person. Approval satisfies a gate. It never rewrites policy.

🧾

The receipts

Every allow, hold, deny and outcome — hash-chained and cryptographically signed. Tear one out and the chain says so. Forge one and the signature says so.

⏻

The breaker

One switch freezes one agent, one industry, or every non-human worker you have. Even pressing the switch leaves a receipt — try the lever above.
