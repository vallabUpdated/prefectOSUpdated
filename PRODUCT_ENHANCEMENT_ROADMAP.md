# Prefect OS Product Enhancement Roadmap

Prefect OS has been enhanced from an AI app builder into an Enterprise AI Governance OS concept for regulated industries.

## New Positioning

**Prefect OS: The Enterprise AI Governance OS for regulated agentic work.**

The platform should govern AI agents, regulatory workflows, approvals, audit evidence, impact analysis, and enterprise knowledge.

## Added UI Modules

1. **Governance**
   - Enterprise risk score
   - Audit readiness metrics
   - AI governance control coverage
   - Governed AI transaction lifecycle
   - AI agent workforce management

2. **Regulatory**
   - Regulation-to-implementation workflow
   - Obligation register
   - Domain agent team
   - One-click launch of regulatory impact workflow prompt

3. **Knowledge Graph**
   - Reference relationship chain connecting regulation, process, app, API, DB, control, and owner
   - Enterprise questions the platform should answer

4. **Home updates**
   - Repositioned as Prefect OS enterprise governance platform
   - Added fintech and insurance templates:
     - Regulatory Impact Analyzer
     - Insurance Claims Governance
     - FinTech Release Risk Board

## Recommended Next Backend Enhancements

- Persistent governance store for prompts, models, agents, tools, approvals, risk scores, and evidence.
- Regulatory document ingestion pipeline with obligation extraction.
- Enterprise asset catalog and knowledge graph database.
- Risk scoring service with configurable policy rules.
- Audit evidence generator per run.
- Integrations with Jira, GitHub, Azure DevOps, ServiceNow, SIEM, and GRC tools.

## MVP Priority

1. Prompt/model/agent lineage table.
2. Risk scoring before execution.
3. Regulatory impact workflow prompt templates.
4. Approval gates based on risk level.
5. Audit evidence export for completed runs.
