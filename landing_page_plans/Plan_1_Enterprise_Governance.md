Plan 1 positions **Prefect OS** as the premier enterprise-grade operating system for governing AI agents in strictly regulated domains. Rather than focusing solely on AI capabilities or raw model speed, this plan addresses the top barrier for enterprise AI adoption: **regulatory compliance, auditability, and catastrophic hallucination prevention**.

## 3. Detailed Section Breakdown & Copy Blueprint

### Hero Section
- **Headline:** Govern Every AI Agent Action. Zero Regulatory Surprises.
- **Subheadline:** Prefect OS gives regulated enterprises total control over agentic workflows with mandatory human-in-the-loop gates, real-time policy scoring, and 100% verifiable audit trails.
- **Primary CTA:** Schedule Executive Briefing (Opens Modal / Calendar)
- **Secondary CTA:** Read Governance Whitepaper (PDF Download)
- **Hero Graphic:** Interactive 3D Mockup of Prefect OS Governance Center showing live transaction risk scoring and approval gates.

### Section 1: Institutional Trust & Pillars
- **Card 1: Hardened Approval Gates (HITL)** — Every high-stakes action requires explicit human sign-off with `interrupt()` checkpoints.
- **Card 2: Dynamic Policy Engine** — Automatically scores transactions against FINRA, HIPAA, and internal compliance rulebooks before execution.
- **Card 3: Cryptographic Audit Ledger** — Immutable record of prompt inputs, agent responses, human approvals, and execution state.

### Section 2: Interactive Risk & Compliance Calculator
- Interactive slider allowing C-suite visitors to select their industry (Banking, Healthcare, Insurance) and monthly AI agent volume.
- Output: Estimated compliance risk reduction (%), audit time saved (hrs/mo), and audit readiness score.

### Section 3: Enterprise Security Specs
- SOC2 Type II Certified infrastructure
- VPC & Air-gapped On-Premises deployment support
- Zero-model-training data pledge
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & SAML/SSO Integration
