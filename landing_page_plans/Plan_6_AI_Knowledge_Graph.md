> **Target Audience:** Enterprise Architects, Chief Data Officers (CDO), GRC Leads, Systems Integrators.
> **Primary Goal:** Position Prefect OS as the central nervous system connecting enterprise regulations, applications, APIs, databases, and AI agents into a unified knowledge graph.
> **Visual Theme:** Cybernetic Dark Glow (`#0B0F19`), Amethyst Purple (`#8B5CF6`), Electric Teal (`#14B8A6`), Interactive Node Network Graph Visuals.

## 1. Executive Strategy & Positioning

Plan 6 positions **Prefect OS** as a holistic **Enterprise Knowledge Graph & Governance Ecosystem**. Rather than treating AI agents as isolated scripts, this landing page demonstrates how Prefect OS connects regulations directly to code implementation, database schema controls, API authorization, and enterprise team ownership.

## 3. Detailed Section Breakdown & Copy Blueprint

### Hero Section
- **Headline:** The Central Nervous System for Enterprise AI Compliance.
- **Subheadline:** Connect regulatory obligations, policy rules, database schemas, APIs, and AI agent workloads into a real-time, auditable knowledge graph.
- **Primary CTA:** Explore Knowledge Graph Architecture (Interactive modal viewer).
- **Secondary CTA:** Read Enterprise Lineage Whitepaper.

### Section 1: The Connected Reference Chain
- **Node 1: Regulation & Law:** EU AI Act, FINRA Rule 3110, HIPAA Privacy Rule.
- **Node 2: Policy Rules:** Configurable risk thresholds and rulebooks (`loan_policy.py`).
- **Node 3: Agentic Workforce:** LangGraph orchestrators, Planner, Spec Writer, Executor agents.
- **Node 4: Data & Systems:** Postgres, Snowflake, REST APIs, Vector DBs.
- **Node 5: Human Owners:** CISOs, Compliance Officers, Lead Engineers.

### Section 2: Enterprise Integration Ecosystem
- Pre-built connectors to Jira, GitHub, Azure DevOps, ServiceNow, SIEM, and GRC systems.
