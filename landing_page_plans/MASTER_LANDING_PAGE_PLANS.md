# Plan 1: Enterprise Trust & Governance First

### Target Audience
Enterprise CISOs, Chief Risk Officers (CRO), Chief Compliance Officers (CCO), Enterprise AI Architects in Regulated Industries (Banking, Insurance, Healthcare).

### Core Messaging & Positioning
Plan 1 positions Prefect OS as an institutional governance platform. It addresses risk reduction, auditability, SOC2/EU AI Act compliance, and human approval gates.

### Layout & Wireframe Architecture
- **Nav Header:** Logo, Solutions, Compliance, Architecture, Executive Briefing Button.
- **Hero:** "Govern Every AI Agent Action. Zero Regulatory Surprises." with live risk score dashboard widget.
- **Trust Banner:** Compliant with FINRA, HIPAA, EU AI Act, ISO 27001.
- **Pillars:** 1) Hardened Approval Gates, 2) Dynamic Policy Engine, 3) Cryptographic Audit Ledger.
- **Interactive Tool:** Executive ROI & Compliance Risk Calculator.
- **Footer:** Schedule Executive Briefing form.
