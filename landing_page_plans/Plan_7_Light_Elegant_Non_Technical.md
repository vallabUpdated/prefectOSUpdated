# Plan 7: Light & Elegant Landing Page (Non-Technical First)

### Target Audience
Business Leaders, Loan Officers, Insurance Claims Managers, Operations Directors, and C-Suite Executives who need to quickly understand Prefect OS capabilities without being overwhelmed by technical jargon, code snippets, or complex architecture diagrams up front.

### Core Strategy & Theme
1. **Light, Premium Design System**: Clean white (`#ffffff`) and soft slate (`#f8fafc`) backdrop with indigo/emerald accents, warm shadows, and glassmorphism cards.
2. **Non-Technical Business Experience Above the Fold**:
   - High-level headline: *"Intelligent Multi-Agent Automation for Loan & Banking Operations"*
   - Jargon-free extraction preview showing verified business fields (Income, DTI, Credit Score).
   - Plain-English 3-Step Workflow (*1. Upload Documents $\rightarrow$ 2. AI Swarm Analysis $\rightarrow$ 3. One-Click Approval*).
   - Solution tiles for Banking, Insurance Claims, and Software Swarms.
   - Interactive ROI & Cost Reduction Calculator.
3. **Technical Architecture Deep-Dive Further Down**:
   - For developers, technical leads, and security auditors, a dedicated section lower down the page presents multi-agent topology node graphs, LangGraph state engine telemetry, SHA-256 audit ledgers, and SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001 compliance badges.
