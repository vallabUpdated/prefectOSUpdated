# Landing Page Plan 5: Minimalist High-Conversion SaaS

> **Target Audience:** Fast-growing AI startups, SaaS Founders, CTOs needing rapid compliance deployment.
> **Primary Goal:** Maximum conversion rate (15%+ conversion) into free-tier signups and self-serve trials.
> **Visual Theme:** Stark Pitch Black (`#050505`), Electric Emerald (`#00F59B`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Bold Typography, Zero Distractions.

## 1. Executive Strategy & Positioning

Plan 5 strips away complex corporate menus, secondary whitepapers, and unnecessary links to focus on **one single goal: immediate trial sign-up**. Designed with ultra-clean minimalist aesthetics and bold, high-contrast typography, this landing page delivers an unmistakable value proposition in under 3 seconds of reading time.

## 3. Detailed Section Breakdown & Copy Blueprint

### Hero Section
- **Headline:** Ship Regulated AI Agents in Days, Not Months.
- **Subheadline:** Add enterprise policy scoring, budget caps, and human-in-the-loop gates to your AI agents with one line of code.
- **Single Input Funnel:** Direct email input box + "Start Free Trial" button right in the hero section.

### Section 1: How It Works (3 Steps)
1. **Pip Install:** `pip install prefect-os`
2. **Attach Guardrails:** Wrap agent calls in `@governed(risk_threshold=0.2)`
3. **Sleep Soundly:** Prefect OS handles all logging, human sign-offs, and compliance evidence.
