# Skill: Regulatory Intelligence for FinTech and Insurance

Use this skill when a task mentions regulations, policies, audit findings, compliance remediation, RBI, IRDAI, SEBI, PCI DSS, GDPR, SOC2, ISO 27001, DORA, KYC, AML, claims governance, underwriting governance, or release risk.

## Objectives

1. Ingest regulation, circular, internal policy, or audit finding.
2. Extract obligations, deadlines, affected parties, and required controls.
3. Map each obligation to impacted applications, APIs, databases, reports, business processes, and owners.
4. Calculate risk using customer impact, data sensitivity, regulatory severity, financial exposure, model confidence, and control coverage.
5. Generate implementation stories, validation checks, approval gates, and audit evidence requirements.
6. Produce an explainable decision record that can be reviewed by compliance, risk, security, engineering, and audit teams.

## Required Output Structure

- Executive summary
- Obligation register
- Impact map
- Risk score and rationale
- Control requirements
- Implementation backlog
- Human approvals required
- Test and evidence plan
- Audit-ready decision log

## Governance Rules

- Do not auto-approve high-risk changes.
- Flag PII, payment data, health data, customer financial data, and model-driven customer decisions.
- Require human review for regulatory ambiguity, high financial impact, production deployment, or customer-facing AI decisions.
- Preserve traceability from source regulation to implementation task to validation evidence.
